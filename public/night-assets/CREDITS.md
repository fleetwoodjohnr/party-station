# Dead of Night — art credits

Everything in this folder is released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication):
no attribution is required, but credit where it's due.

- **Models:** [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), downloaded from [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza), where each page lists the licence as CC0 1.0.
- **Textures:** [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (all CC0).

How they were changed: clips pruned to the ones the game uses, meshes simplified (meshoptimizer) and quantized, multi-material props flattened to vertex colours, and textures shrunk to 256 px (detail layers turned grey).
The originals are at the links below.
